docs: Say what the audit runs beside, not as part of, and how long it takes

Six documentation fixes from the last review, all of them places where the
prose had drifted from what the code does:

- README no longer reads as though `npm run test:e2e` runs the audit: it is a
  separate command, which is the whole point of it not being in the suite.
- AGENTS.md gives the audit's real runtime — about three and a half minutes
  locally, more on CI's runners — rather than the five to eight it had before
  the walk got faster.
- The audit's header, and AGENTS.md with it, now say its settings check runs
  before the app loads and again on every tab, which is what it does.
- The audit job's own "keep in step" note points at the browser-tests job
  above it, not at nothing below it.
- The audit job's comment no longer promises that a slow walk always prints
  the entries that matched nothing: if the test's own timeout fires, Playwright
  reports a timeout.
